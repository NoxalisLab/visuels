Your ISP box Wi-Fi name helps Google and Apple locate phones nearby.

Add _nomap at the end of the SSID. Keep the network visible (not hidden).

#wifi #privacy
---
How:
1. Open your box Wi-Fi settings
2. Rename e.g. Home → Home_nomap (no space)
3. Leave SSID visible — a hidden name makes Apple's opt-out ineffective
4. Reconnect every device

Official: Google Maps Help + Apple Wi-Fi positioning.
